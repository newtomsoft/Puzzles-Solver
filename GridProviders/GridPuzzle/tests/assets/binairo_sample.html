How to play Binairo

The Binairo puzzle is played on a grid where the goal is to fill each cell with either a black or a white circle. The rules are: every row and every column must contain an equal number of black and white circles (or one more of one color in odd-sized grids). You can never have more than two adjacent circles of the same color in a row or column. Lastly, each row must be unique, and each column must also be unique in its arrangement of black and white circles.

How to play Kin-Kon-Kan

Kin-Kon-Kan is a logic puzzle published by Nikoli. The task consists of a grid divided into regions. The goal is to fill in some cells with diagonal lines ("mirrors").

  1. Each region contains exactly one mirror.

  2. Every mirror must reflect at least one laser beam.

  3. Letter-number pairs at the edges of the grid can be connected by straight lines ("laser beams") that bounce of the same number of mirrors as the number in the letter-number pair.

Read more: Kin-Kon-Kan rules & tips

Kin-Kon-Kan Puzzle Solving Tips & Techniques:

  1. Start with Extremes: Look for clues with "0" or the highest numbers. A "0" means the two points of the same letter see each other in a straight line with no mirrors in between—all cells on that direct line must be empty of mirrors. High numbers often force longer, winding paths.

  2. Corner and Edge Logic: A beam entering a corner cell from the outside must immediately reflect if a mirror is present. This can quickly define mirror orientation in that region.

  3. Region Deduction: Since each region has exactly one mirror, if you can deduce that a certain cell cannot have a mirror, that restricts possibilities for other cells in the same region.

  4. Beam Tracing: Mentally trace possible paths for beams, especially those with low numbers (1, 2). Look for unique ways they can achieve their required reflections given the positions of other mirrors/empty cells.

  5. Mirror Necessity: If a region's only possible mirror positions are all in cells that no laser beam could possibly reach, your assumption is wrong. Every mirror must be hit by a beam.

  6. Intersection & Exclusion: Beams can cross without interacting. Use confirmed empty cells (from "0" clues or failed path attempts) to limit the possible paths for other beams.

  7. Double-Check Mirror Counts: Periodically verify that the reflection count for each active beam matches its clue. Placing a mirror often affects multiple beams.
